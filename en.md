# EasyDCA: privacy and terms

*[Version française](index.md)*

EasyDCA is an Android app that helps you invest regularly (dollar-cost averaging, DCA), stick to
your long-term investment plan and stay motivated with your friends. **It is not investment
advice**: the app does not manage money and does not place any order.

- [Privacy policy](privacy.md): what data, why, where, for how long, your rights.
- [Terms of use](terms.md): what the app does, what it does not do, what we expect from you.
- [Delete your account](account-deletion.md): in the app, or by e-mail without the app.
