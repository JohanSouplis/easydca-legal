# Delete your EasyDCA account

*[Version française](suppression-du-compte.md)*

## In the app (immediate)

1. Open EasyDCA and sign in.
2. Tap your avatar at the top of the screen: **Profile and settings**.
3. At the very bottom: **Delete my account**.
4. Read what will be erased, type **DELETE** (in French **SUPPRIMER**), then **Delete for good**.

Your account and your data are erased from the server right away, then from your phone, and you are
signed out.

## Without the app (by e-mail)

If you no longer have the app or your phone, write to **investisseurlogique@gmail.com**
with the subject "Delete my EasyDCA account", **from the address of the Google account** you used in
EasyDCA (or mention it in the message). Your account is deleted within **30 days at most**, and you
receive a confirmation.

## What is erased

Everything about your account, permanently: your sign-in account, your plan and its versions, your
DCA and its validation history, your XP, streak, milestones and Pépite, your portfolio values, your
change reasons, your Online profile (nickname, friend code), your friends and friend requests, your
blocks, your referral, your shares, your place in your team, your invitations and encouragements.

## What remains

- XP your sponsor already earned thanks to you stays on their account, with no link to yours.
- A team you created stays with its other members (without a creator); it disappears if you were
  its last member.
- Nothing else is kept: no separate backup, no copy.

You can come back with the same Google account, but you will start from scratch.
